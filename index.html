ABOUT ME

I'm an experienced Instructional Designer & eLearning Developer with 5+ years of expertise in creating interactive, learner-centered training solutions. My work combines solid instructional design foundations—ADDIE, SAM, and Kirkpatrick's models—with technical proficiency in a wide range of authoring tools, including Articulate Storyline, Adobe Captivate, Xyleme, Camtasia, and the Adobe Creative Cloud Suite.

I specialize in building SCORM-compliant modules for LMS integration, transforming complex concepts into engaging digital learning experiences. Whether collaborating with cross-functional teams or developing courses independently, I bring both creative design thinking and technical precision to every project.

I also build AI directly into my production pipeline — from dual-tool drafting (ChatGPT + Claude) to ElevenLabs voice and lip-sync synthesis — while keeping protected source material inside the data-governance boundaries healthcare ID requires. Details in my AI workflow case study.

Code Blue: Simulated Cardiac Arrest

Scenario-based clinical simulation with two branching pathways (in-hospital ACLS + out-of-hospital BLS) and 17 graded decision points.

Perioperative Series: Surgical Safety

Gamified Jeopardy-format simulation for perioperative nurses — learner competes live against three AI-driven nurse opponents across 5 categories × 5 questions.
Design decision: rebuilt a text-heavy PDF + standard MCQ assessment into a game-show format to fix the engagement gap typical of compliance training.
Every question — correct or incorrect — triggers an explanation; built around retrieval practice and immediate-feedback principles.
Storyline 360 with custom buzzer mechanics and live scoreboard; visuals co-designed with a graphic specialist.
AI-assisted voiceover via ElevenLabs for the three opponents; live human host where emotional delivery matters.
Lead ID on a 2-person team; end-to-end from instructional rebuild to SCORM publish.

Critical Care Series: Fluids, Electrolytes, and Homeostasis

Course of the Year · NPS 80+ — diagnosis-driven simulation on fluids, electrolytes, and homeostasis for critical-care nurses. Learners interpret ECG strips and lab panels to reach the correct clinical assessment.
Design decision: evidence-first answering — learners must work through the strip + labs before answer options surface, modeling real clinical reasoning instead of option-elimination.
Assessment blueprint mapped to Bloom's L1–L4.
Adobe-composed clinical visuals (ECG strips, lab panels).
AI-assisted voiceover via ElevenLabs.
Lead ID on a team with SME and graphic specialist; end-to-end from storyboard to SCORM publish.
